How it works

Three steps to clarity

From raw reviews to a ranked action list — in under a minute. No setup, no integrations required to start.

Step 01

Drop in your app URL

Paste your App Store or Google Play link. Rayzoai fetches all public reviews automatically — no API keys, no developer account needed.

Step 02

AI clusters by problem type

Our model reads every review and groups them into semantic categories — crashes, UX friction, missing features, billing issues — with frequency scores.

Step 03

Get a ranked fix list

Receive a prioritized report with severity scores, example quotes, and the exact issues to tackle first for maximum rating improvement.
